Data Gathering Process

The usability testing session is designed to let respondents explore the improved Fit-Balance prototype, complete basic tasks, and share feedback about the system’s clarity, ease of use, and usefulness.

  • Introduce the purpose of the evaluation.
  • Ask the respondent to review the consent form.
  • Collect basic respondent information without unnecessary personal data.
  • Let respondents perform selected prototype tasks.
  • Collect SUS ratings and open-ended feedback.

Testing Tasks

Suggested respondent tasks

Task 1: Explore the dashboard

Ask the respondent to identify where they can view their progress, goal, and available workout/nutrition options.

Task 2: Select a fitness goal

Ask the respondent to choose a goal such as Muscle Gain or Fat Loss and observe if the options are understandable.

Task 3: Review workout screens

Ask the respondent to open a workout routine and check if the flow gives enough control, feedback, and freedom.

Task 4: Give feedback

Ask the respondent to rate the prototype using SUS and answer short open-ended questions.
