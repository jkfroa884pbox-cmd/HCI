Main Findings

Heuristic evaluation results

Use the filter to focus on high, medium, or low priority issues.

Screen/AreaIssue IdentifiedRelated HeuristicPriorityRecommendation
Goal ScreenUsers may need clearer guidance when choosing between fitness goals.Match between system and real worldHighUse familiar goal labels and short descriptions for Muscle Gain, Fat Loss, Push/Pull, and Full-Body routines.
Workout ScreenThe flow needs stronger user control when reviewing or changing workout selections.User control and freedomHighAdd Back, Edit, Cancel, and confirmation actions so users can recover from wrong choices.
DashboardProgress and current goal may not be immediately visible.Visibility of system statusMediumShow goal status, calorie target, and workout progress using clear summary cards.
Nutrition TrackingUsers may be unsure if food or calorie entries were saved successfully.Visibility of system statusMediumAdd confirmation messages such as “Meal added” or “Calories updated.”
Forms/InputThere may be limited prevention for invalid or incomplete fitness data.Error preventionMediumUse required fields, helper text, and input limits for age, weight, height, and calorie values.
Visual DesignSome sections may feel visually heavy if cards and spacing are inconsistent.Aesthetic and minimalist designLowStandardize spacing, typography, icons, and button styles across all screens.
Applied Improvements

Prototype revisions after evaluation

✓

Goal Screen Added

A dedicated goal screen was added to support clearer goal selection and guide users toward suitable workout paths.

✓

Workout Screen Reviewed

The workout screens were evaluated and refined to improve user control, freedom, and clarity during routine selection.

✓

Navigation and Feedback Improved

Recommendations focused on making the interface more understandable through clearer labels, visual feedback, and consistent actions.
