Recommendations

Design improvements for Fit-Balance

The recommendations below are based on the heuristic findings and are organized by priority so the team can decide what to refine first.

Priority Fixes

Recommended design actions

High Priority

Strengthen user control in workout screens

Add Back, Edit, Cancel, and confirmation options so users can correct choices without restarting the entire flow.

High Priority

Improve goal selection clarity

Keep the goal screen simple, with short descriptions and clear visual differences between Muscle Gain, Fat Loss, Push/Pull, and Full-Body options.

Medium Priority

Add feedback and status messages

Use messages such as “Goal saved,” “Workout selected,” or “Meal added” to reassure users that an action was successful.

Medium Priority

Standardize visual hierarchy

Use consistent button colors, card spacing, icons, and text sizes across the dashboard, workout, and nutrition pages.

Low Priority

Prepare accessibility refinements

Check color contrast, readable text size, touch-friendly button dimensions, and simple labels for mobile use.

Conclusion

Overall evaluation conclusion

The Fit-Balance prototype shows strong potential as an integrated fitness and nutrition management system because it focuses on meaningful user needs such as setting goals, organizing workouts, and tracking calorie-related progress. The heuristic evaluation helped identify specific areas that affect usability, especially the need for clearer guidance, consistent feedback, and stronger user control.

After applying the recommendations, the prototype is now more prepared for usability testing with actual respondents. The next step is to collect SUS scores and open-ended feedback, compare the results with the heuristic findings, and continue refining the prototype based on user-centered evidence.
